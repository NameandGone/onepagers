# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People who handle specialized documents for a living: lending and closing teams, legal reviewers, and tax professionals. They are trying to get a narrow, repetitive PDF job done without losing the source context or spending an afternoon retyping it.

## Product Purpose

onepagers is the home for focused browser tools that turn document-heavy work into checked, usable data. The landing page should explain the job in plain language, put the tools in reach immediately, and show enough of the real workflow for a first-time visitor to trust the handoff.

## Brand Personality

Direct, human, and badass. The voice is short, plainspoken, specific, and confident without pretending the tools do more than they do.

## Anti-references

Avoid complicated product language, a hero banner that delays the tool, decorative floating document artwork, crowded navigation, sloppy spacing, generic SaaS promises, stock icons, and calls to action that rely on ornamental arrows instead of clear words.

## Design Principles

- Explain the job before the brand story.
- Put the useful thing in reach on the first screen.
- Keep the source document connected to the extracted result.
- Make narrow tools feel intentional, not overbuilt.
- Be honest about constraints and let the product proof do the selling.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA basics: readable contrast, keyboard-accessible links and controls, semantic headings, descriptive iframe titles, and answers that remain present in the initial HTML. Respect prefers-reduced-motion so animated copy becomes immediate and calm.
